What this is

We looked at Hiki the way a new member does, then rebuilt the moments we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion and revenue across the market, not inside one app. We pointed that at Hiki: read your App Store listing and your in-app purchase list, read your site, studied your screens, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people before they decide.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: your pink and lavender, your handwritten headlines, your trait chips, your profile card, your photography. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the moment of decision.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
