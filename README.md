# Growth Audit — Hiki: Autism ADHD & ND Dating

**Live:** https://KseniyaSim.github.io/hiki-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas for Hiki's onboarding and paywall, each rebuilt as a working screen in Hiki's own design: the pink and lavender, the handwritten headlines, the trait chips, the profile card and the member photography. Only the order changes.

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Offer the $34 trial to people who say no | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 2 | Name what they came for on the paywall | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 3 | Show people their profile before the price | Loader, Profile | CR +10–15% |
| 4 | Put your community next to the price | Paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 5 | Ask for location after the paywall | Location | CR +10–15% |

Combined potential: CR to purchase up to +25%, ARPU up to +30%.

**Grounding.** Every price, member count, quote and Premium feature comes from Hiki's own App Store listing, its in-app purchase list and hikiapp.com. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Hiki.
